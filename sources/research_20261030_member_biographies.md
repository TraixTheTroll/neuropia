# Sources for the Torbjørn Kråkenes and Lars Bø biographies

Verified on 30 October 2026. These notes support the public About text in `_pages/member-torbjorn-krakenes.md` and `_pages/member-lars-bo.md`. They are an editorial record, not a comprehensive literature review. The website excludes `sources/` from deployment.

## Method and scope

The research-lookup skill's optional Parallel and OpenRouter API credentials were unavailable. Public web search and direct inspection of University of Bergen, Helse Bergen, journal, and PubMed pages were used instead. Searches combined each full name with terms including `researcher`, `PhD`, `doctorate`, `multiple sclerosis`, `mesenchymal`, and `extracellular vesicles`; focused follow-ups checked RAM-MS, SMART-MS, and cortical demyelination.

Biographical facts were checked against institutional records. Research summaries reflect documented publications and study descriptions. Authors' participation in a publication is not treated as proof of a specific clinical leadership role or personal proficiency with every method. No treatment benefit is inferred from study participation.

## Torbjørn Kråkenes

1. [UiB doctoral announcement: Fjerneffekt av kreft](https://www.uib.no/nye-doktorgrader/143221/fjerneffekt-av-kreft), published 4 March 2021 and updated 6 April 2021. Confirms a BSc in nanotechnology and an MSc in medical cell biology at UiB, and the 16 March 2021 doctoral defence. Describes research on the cellular targets of Yo antibodies, CDR2L/CDR2 localisation, and Purkinje cell cultures. Supports the education and experimental-neurobiology background in the biography.
2. [UiB department notice confirming the completed defence](https://k1nytt.w.uib.no/2021/03/19/ph-d-hjornet-68/), 19 March 2021. Confirms that the defence took place, rather than relying only on the earlier announcement.
3. [Current UiB employee profile](https://www4.uib.no/finn-ansatte/torbjorn.krakenes). Lists a guest-researcher affiliation in neurobiology in the Department of Clinical Medicine. This institutional designation does not replace the group's separately supplied senior-researcher and basic-research PI roles. The Norwegian profile was accessible; the corresponding English URL failed during lookup, so the verified Norwegian URL is used for the public institutional-profile link.
4. [Helse Bergen: the MS Node](https://www.helse-bergen.no/en/neuro-sysmed-english/research-groups/the-ms-node/). Lists Kråkenes among Neuro-SysMed researchers. The page contains some historical member titles; it was used to corroborate affiliation, not to overwrite current group-supplied roles.
5. [Kråkenes et al., PLOS ONE, 2023](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0293908). DOI: [10.1371/journal.pone.0293908](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0293908); PMID: [37943848](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37943848/). Supports research on MSCs in a mouse demyelination model. Kråkenes's listed contributions include investigation, methodology, conceptualisation, and analysis; Bø contributed to conceptualisation and methodology. The profile describes the research topic without claiming established clinical efficacy.
6. [Kråkenes et al., International Journal of Molecular Sciences, 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39408622/). DOI: [10.3390/ijms251910292](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijms251910292). A review of MSC-derived vesicles as a potential MS treatment, co-authored by Kråkenes and Bø. Supports the EV research focus; it is not presented as an original therapeutic experiment.
7. [Kvistad et al., Neurology, 2026: SMART-MS](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42081777/). DOI: [10.1212/WNL.0000000000214915](https://doi.org/10.1212/WNL.0000000000214915). Confirms Kråkenes and Bø as co-authors of the trial investigating intrathecal MSCs in progressive MS. The biographies say they contributed; neither is assigned the clinical PI role from author order. No efficacy claim is made.

Neuropia co-leadership with Christopher Elnan Kvistad and responsibility for laboratory-based basic research were supplied by the group earlier in this conversation and preserved from the existing page. The closing connection between experimental and clinical work is an editorial synthesis of the sources above.

## Lars Bø

1. [Current UiB employee profile](https://www4.uib.no/en/find-employees/lars.bo). Confirms professor, Department of Clinical Medicine, Faculty of Medicine. Provides the public institutional-profile link.
2. [Helse Bergen: national MS network core group](https://www.helse-bergen.no/nasjonal-kompetansetjeneste-for-multippel-sklerose-ms/ansatte-ved-nasjonal-kompetansetjeneste-for-multippel-sklerose-ms/), updated 1 June 2026. Confirms his consultant position at Haukeland's Department of Neurology and professorship at UiB. Other organisational responsibilities are omitted to keep the biography focused.
3. [UiB doctoral announcement, November 1998](https://ka.uib.no/doktorgrader/1998/november/bo/bo.html), cross-checked against the doctorate listed in his current UiB profile. Confirms medical training at UiB and the 1998 doctorate on MS immunopathology. Describes inflammation, myelin damage, and axonal injury. The biography uses “doctorate” for the historical dr.med. qualification.
4. [Bø et al., Journal of Neuropathology and Experimental Neurology, 2003](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12901699/). DOI: [10.1093/jnen/62.7.723](https://doi.org/10.1093/jnen/62.7.723). The study characterises cortical subpial demyelination in MS. The direct PubMed page returned a sparse response during one visit; article metadata and the abstract were corroborated through search and the [publisher's paper](https://academic.oup.com/jnen/article-pdf/62/7/723/9553668/62-7-723.pdf). Supports the description of his cortical/grey-matter pathology research.
5. [Helse Bergen: RAM-MS](https://www.helse-bergen.no/en/neuro-sysmed-english/clinical-studies-at-neuro-sysmed/ms--clinical-studies/ram-ms/), updated 6 July 2026. Explicitly identifies Bø as study director. Describes the comparison of autologous haematopoietic stem cell transplantation with high-efficacy MS therapies. The biography does not infer an outcome or claim that recruitment remains open.
6. The PLOS ONE 2023 and SMART-MS 2026 papers listed above support his contributions to preclinical MSC and clinical treatment research. The final paragraph is an editorial synthesis of those documented areas, not a claim of a new Neuropia appointment or a specific unpublished project.

## Excluded or limited evidence

- A publisher's author biography surfaced in search, but its surrounding text mixed information about different co-authors. Education and doctoral details were taken from UiB instead.
- ResearchGate and automated bibliometric profiles were not used to substantiate appointments, degrees, or quantitative impact claims.
- A social-media post about hybrid nanoparticle funding was not needed for the final biographies and no funding or award claim was added.
- Neither author counts nor historical research-group totals were copied into personal profiles.
